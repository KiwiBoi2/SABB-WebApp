Welcome

We are the St. Andrew's Big Band!

We, the St Andrew's Big Band are a non-profit musical ensemble dedicated to sharing both jazz classics, as well as more contemporary takes on the genre, while supporting charities with scopes both small and large. Composed of brilliant volunteer musical alumni from across Auckland, we combine professional performance etiquette and entertainment to bring engaging performances to our local community.

First established in 2011, by our director Hamish Arthur, the band has continuously operated for 15 years. Every event serves a dual purpose: to keep the spirit of jazz classics alive, while raising funds for local charities. All profits from tickets go directly to supporting these charities. It is through these concerts that our members are able to give back to the community that has supported them through their musical careers, as well as do some good for those in need.
